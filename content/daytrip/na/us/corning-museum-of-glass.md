Partly a history museum, partly a technology museum, partly an art museum, the Corning Museum of Glass has 4,000 year old glass artifacts, glass that was made by volcanoes, lightning, and sponge organisms, a film of an enormous glass bottle-making machine, live demos of glassblowing and flameworking, a 21-ton observatory mirror blank that didn't work, the largest American cut-glass punch bowl ever made, and a chandelier made of blue PV butterflies that supplies its own power.

And you can sign up for a supervised session where you make your own glass souvenir by blowing, flameworking, or sandblasting.
