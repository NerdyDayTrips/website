Located in Seattle's International District, the Seattle Pinball Museum features more than 50 pinball machines available for unlimited play with a single admission fee. The collection includes vintage and modern games spanning several decades, allowing visitors to compare different eras of pinball design and technology. The museum is suitable for families, casual players, and dedicated pinball enthusiasts, and highlights the history and artwork of pinball in a hands-on setting.
