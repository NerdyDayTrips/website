The Nonni’s House in Akureyri is a historic museum housed in one of the town’s oldest wooden buildings (circa 1850), commemorating Jón “Nonni” Sveinsson, the celebrated children’s author and Jesuit priest. Visitors can explore the childhood home where Nonni grew up, view original household items and multilingual editions of his beloved books, and gain insight into 19th-century Icelandic life.
