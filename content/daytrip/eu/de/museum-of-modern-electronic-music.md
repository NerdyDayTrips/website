The Museum of Modern Electronic Music (MOMEM) is a first-of-its-kind cultural space celebrating the evolution of electronic dance music. Designed as a hybrid between museum, club, and creative hub, it features immersive exhibits, live DJ workshops, film screenings, and discussions on electronic music culture. It spotlights Frankfurt’s techno legacy while engaging visitors with interactive events and performances.
