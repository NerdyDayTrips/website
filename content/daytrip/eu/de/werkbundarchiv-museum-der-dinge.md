The Museum der Dinge in Berlin explores the history of modern design and mass-produced objects from the 20th and 21st centuries. It reflects on the ideas of the Deutscher Werkbund, founded in 1907 to promote quality, functionality, and sustainability in industrial design and architecture. The museum features a unique collection of documents and objects, an open depot exhibition, and special programs for those interested in the past and present of design.
