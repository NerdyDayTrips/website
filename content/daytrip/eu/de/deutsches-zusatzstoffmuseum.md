The German Additives Museum in Hamburg explores the thousands of additives used in food production. Visitors learn about their functions, manufacturing processes, and potential risks. Through everyday products, the museum introduces emulsifiers, stabilizers, colorings, preservatives, flavorings, baking agents, and taste enhancers.
