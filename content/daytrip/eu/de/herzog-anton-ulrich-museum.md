The Herzog Anton Ulrich Museum in Braunschweig, founded in 1754, is one of Europe’s oldest art museums, renowned for its outstanding collection of Old Master paintings, featuring artists like Cranach, Dürer, Rubens, Rembrandt, and a rare Vermeer, alongside sculptures, decorative arts, and a major print and drawing collection. Housed in a majestic Italian Renaissance-style building, with a modern extension, it offers over 4,000 artworks spanning 3,000 years
