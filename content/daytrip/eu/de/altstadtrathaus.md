Discover the permanent exhibition in Braunschweig’s Old Town Hall, housed in a beautifully preserved medieval building from the 13th–15th centuries. In its vaulted basement, the exhibition traces the city’s development from Hanseatic power through ducal court life to industrialization, featuring treasures such as Duke Henry the Lion’s seal, Frederick the Great’s flute, early cameras, motorcycles, and 19th‑century machinery.
