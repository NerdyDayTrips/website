Experience nature from a new perspective on the treetop path in Bad Harzburg. This 1,000-meter-long walkway rises up to 26 meters above the forest floor, offering stunning views of the Harz landscape. Along the way, interactive stations provide insights into local wildlife, plants, and forest ecology.
